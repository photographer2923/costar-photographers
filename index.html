Use a pseudonym. Protect your identity.

  • To join Discord, create an account using a pseudonym. The email address you use to sign up is not visible to other members or server admins.
  • Choose a display name and profile picture that do not identify you. Avoid handles you use publicly elsewhere.
  • On Signal, review Settings → Privacy → Phone Number. Set both who can see your number and who can find you by number to “Nobody.” Signal still requires a phone number to register.
  • Keep names, locations, identifying assignment details, and personal information out of messages and images.

No usernames or member lists are displayed on this website. Inside either chat, other members can see your display name and profile.
